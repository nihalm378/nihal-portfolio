Web dashboard

A whole company on one screen.

HR teams open AiTIME to answer one question first: who is here. The dashboard answers that with live counters and a running punch feed, then gives trends and location underneath for anyone who needs more.

I designed the dashboard and the screens across attendance, scheduling, leave and reports, and worked on the software development across the product.

  • 01Live present, absent and late counters fed by biometric devices and the app
  • 02Shift scheduling, leave and approvals in the same navigation
  • 03Reports that export cleanly to Excel and PDF
Mobile app

One big button, because that is the job.

Most employees open the app to do a single thing: punch in. The home screen is built around that action, with geofence and offline status shown before they tap.

I designed the app, developed it in Flutter and published it as AiTime Cloud on the App Store and Google Play.
