lokiz.org
Brand

Hardware that looks like one family.

LOKIZ sells magnetic locks, keypads, readers and smart locks to installers and contractors. The products came from different lines and were photographed differently, so the catalogue felt like several brands.

I set a single look and applied it everywhere: the indigo, how the logo sits on a product shot, the same soft studio light and angle for every item.

  • 01Visual identity and logo usage
  • 02Product photography retouching and optimisation for web and marketplaces
  • 03Campaign visuals, packaging, brochures and data sheets
  • 04Amazon listings with A+ content
Website

A catalogue people can actually browse.

The site at lokiz.org organises the range by category, with consistent imagery and a direct route to a quote on WhatsApp, which is how this market prefers to buy.
