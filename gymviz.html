The problem

A front desk has seconds, not minutes.

The earlier dashboard was a wall of tables. Everything was there, but staff had to read it to find out how the day was going.

The redesign puts four numbers first: people in the gym, revenue today, cash in hand and active members. Everything else is one click away.

  • 01Card-based overview that reads at a glance from across the desk
  • 02Light and dark themes built on one set of design tokens
  • 03Quick actions for what staff repeat all day: add member, take payment, sell plan
Member app

The gym in a pocket.

Members check in with a QR code, book classes, follow workouts and manage their plan. I designed every screen in Figma, built the app in Flutter and published it on both stores.
